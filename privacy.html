Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

ROAD IRON sells new old stock (NOS), used, and vintage automotive parts online. This policy explains how information is handled when you browse, contact us, or place an order.

Information collected

We may collect your name, email address, shipping and billing details, order history, product inquiries, and messages you submit. The website and its service providers may process technical information such as device, browser, and usage data to operate and secure the service.

How information is used

We use information to process orders and shipping, answer questions, provide customer support, prevent fraud, maintain the website, and meet legal obligations.

Payment and service providers

Payments are processed by Stripe. ROAD IRON does not store full payment card details on its website. We may share necessary order or contact details with payment processors, shipping providers, website hosting and database providers, and other vendors that help fulfill orders and run the site. Those providers handle data under their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

Retention and security

We retain information as reasonably needed for orders, support, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal compliance. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and operational requirements. You may choose not to provide optional information, though some details are necessary to complete an order.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
