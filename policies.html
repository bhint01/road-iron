Store Policies & Customer Support

Information for ROAD IRON shoppers. Please review the specific item listing and checkout details before purchasing.

Item Condition & Fitment

ROAD IRON offers new old stock (NOS), used, vintage and hard-to-find items. Packaging, cosmetic condition, completeness and compatibility may vary. Product descriptions and photographs describe known condition. Buyers should verify part numbers and vehicle fitment before ordering and ask questions through private messaging if unsure.

Shipping

Available shipping services and charges are presented during checkout when carrier rates can be calculated. Oversized or unusual items may require a separate freight arrangement. Processing time, carrier transit time and delivery dates can vary. A tracking number will appear in the customer order area when it has been recorded. Local pickup is offered only where expressly indicated.

Returns, Damaged or Incorrect Items

All sales are final. ROAD IRON does not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, incorrect fitment selected by the buyer, or other non-defective items. Damaged parts are an exception. If a part arrives damaged, contact ROAD IRON promptly through private messaging with your order number and clear photographs of the item and packaging. Do not ship an item back before receiving instructions. If the wrong item was sent or an item was materially misdescribed, contact us so we can review the issue and provide any remedy required by applicable law. This policy does not limit non-waivable consumer rights.

Payment & Orders

Payments for ROAD IRON inventory are processed through a third-party checkout provider. ROAD IRON does not request payment card numbers in website messages. Orders are subject to availability and payment confirmation. Third-party marketplace seller checkout and payouts are not yet enabled.

Privacy

We collect information you provide to process orders, respond to messages, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations. Customer conversations and their photo attachments are accessible only to the account associated with the conversation and authorized ROAD IRON administrators. Payment processors, shipping carriers and website service providers may receive information necessary to perform their services. Do not send sensitive payment information in messages. For privacy questions or requests, contact us through the private messaging page.

Contact & Order Tracking

Sign in to send ROAD IRON a private message or view order status. We do not publish a residential contact address on this page. Additional business contact or return information will be provided when legally required.
