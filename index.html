Store Policies

Shipping

Shipping charges and available delivery methods are shown before payment. Oversized or unusually heavy parts may require a separate freight quote. Local pickup may be arranged when offered on a listing.

Returns & Refunds

Because many Road Iron items are vintage, used, NOS, or one-of-a-kind, buyers should verify fitment and condition before ordering. Any item-specific return terms shown on the listing control. If an item arrives materially different from its description, contact Road Iron promptly.

Privacy

Customer information is used to process orders, provide support, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Payment card details are handled by the payment processor and are not stored by Road Iron.
