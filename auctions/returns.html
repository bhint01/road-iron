Auction returns and delivery issues

Shipped purchases

Report damage, a wrong item, or a material listing mismatch to ROAD IRON within 48 hours after delivery. Change-of-mind sales are final. Contact ROAD IRON through your auction account and include the auction reference, a description of the problem, and photographs when available.

Keep the item and packaging while the claim is reviewed. Do not return an item without instructions from ROAD IRON. A report does not automatically authorize a refund. Approved seller-fault claims include the affected item amount, original shipping and applicable tax; authorized return shipping is handled as part of the claim.

Local pickup

Pickup purchases are final: no returns or refunds. Inspect the item before completing pickup.

This policy does not limit rights or remedies required by applicable law.
