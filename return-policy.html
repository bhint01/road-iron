Returns & Refunds

Read the product description and confirm part numbers, condition, and vehicle fitment before placing an order.

Automotive parts

Used, vintage, new old stock (NOS), rare and one-of-a-kind automotive parts are generally final sale. We do not ordinarily accept returns or exchanges for change of mind or buyer-selected fitment errors.

Damaged, incorrect or misdescribed items

If an item arrives damaged, the wrong item is delivered, or an item is materially misdescribed, contact ROAD IRON promptly through private messaging. Include your order number and photographs of the item and packaging so we can review the issue and applicable remedies. Do not ship an item back until you receive return instructions.

Other merchandise

New clothing and other categories may have different return or exchange terms. Any applicable terms will be stated on the product listing and at purchase. A general return window for these categories has not yet been established.

Refunds and legal rights

Where a refund is approved, ROAD IRON will provide instructions and arrange the applicable refund through the original payment channel where possible. This policy does not limit rights or remedies that cannot be waived under applicable law.

For shipping, privacy and payment details, see our full store policies.
