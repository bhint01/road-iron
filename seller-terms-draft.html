Third-Party Seller Terms — DRAFT

Not yet in effect. These are proposed terms for review by ROAD IRON's owner and legal adviser. The third-party marketplace is not enabled for transactions. No seller should rely on this page as a final agreement.

1. Eligibility and accounts

Sellers must provide accurate contact, business, and identity information; maintain control of their accounts; and satisfy any verification required by the payment provider and applicable law. ROAD IRON may approve, decline, or suspend applications and listings.

2. Listings and lawful goods

Sellers must own or have authority to sell listed goods, describe their actual condition, disclose known defects, use their own or authorized photos, and state accurate part numbers and fitment information. Counterfeit, stolen, prohibited, recalled or otherwise unlawful goods are not permitted. ROAD IRON may remove noncompliant listings.

3. Marketplace fee — proposed

The proposed platform commission is 8% of the item sale price, excluding separately charged shipping. Any payment processing charges, refunds, taxes, disputes, or adjustments must be described in the final signed agreement before seller checkout is activated. ROAD IRON-owned inventory is not subject to this marketplace commission.

4. Shipping, returns, and disputes

Sellers would be responsible for accurate shipping details, secure packing, timely dispatch, delivery evidence, item-specific return disclosures, and resolving legitimate customer issues. Applicable consumer laws override any inconsistent listing terms. A final agreement must establish deadlines and how returns, chargebacks, and damaged shipments are handled.

5. Payments and taxes

Seller payouts will require a supported payment-provider onboarding process and identity verification. No third-party payout functionality is currently live. Sellers are responsible for their own tax obligations except where the platform is legally required to collect or remit taxes.

6. Intellectual property and privacy

Sellers must have rights to their listing content and grant ROAD IRON permission to display it to operate the marketplace. Sellers may use customer information only as necessary to fulfill orders and comply with law, and must not misuse or disclose it.

7. Suspension and account closure

ROAD IRON may pause listings or accounts to address fraud, safety, nonperformance, or policy violations, subject to the final agreement and applicable law.

8. Items requiring final owner/legal approval

Before launch, finalize seller identity and legal entity, governing law, payout timing, fee deductions, cancellation and return periods, dispute resolution, indemnification, limitation of liability, insurance requirements, record retention, and seller consent/version tracking.
